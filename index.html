The 1985 original that started it all. The Chicago colorway features the iconic red, white, and black that defined basketball footwear history. Worn by Michael Jordan during his rookie season, this silhouette revolutionized the game with its premium leather construction and Air cushioning technology. The "Banned" story only added to its legendary status, making it one of the most sought-after sneakers of all time.
