AIR JORDAN 4

RETRO

YELLOW THUNDER

Bold yellow accents electrify the classic AJ4 silhouette in this 2023 release. The nubuck upper features vibrant Tour Yellow on the mesh wings, eyelets, and midsole. Black accents provide contrast while white on the outsole balances the design. Originally inspired by lightning and thunder themes, this colorway brings energy to Tinker Hatfield's 1989 masterpiece with visible Air cushioning in the heel.
